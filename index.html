Audio2110

ST 2110-30 and AES67 audio for Mac

Audio2110 is a network audio driver for macOS. It connects Mac audio applications directly to a broadcast facility’s SMPTE ST 2110-30 and AES67 networks. Any app that uses CoreAudio, such as a DAW, a playout system or a monitoring tool, sees Audio2110 as an ordinary audio interface.

Audio2110 is developed by Austin Storms for in-house use at a broadcast facility. It is not sold or distributed to the public.

What it does

  • Virtual audio devices. Multi-channel CoreAudio devices at 48 or 96 kHz, created and configured at runtime.
  • Senders and receivers. Streams audio to and from the network, with SDP, SAP and RAVENNA (mDNS/RTSP) discovery.
  • Locked to the facility clock. The device clock follows PTPv2, the network’s media clock, so audio is never resampled.
  • Redundant networks. Receives on both networks at once and merges them (SMPTE ST 2022-7), so losing one network causes no audible glitch.
  • Sample-aligned streams. Streams that carry the same media time reach applications on the same audio frame, across streams and across devices.
  • Central management. Senders, receivers and routing are managed from the facility’s broadcast control system.

Built on Apple’s modern driver platform

The audio devices come from a DriverKit audio driver that runs in user space, isolated from the rest of the system. Installation needs one approval in System Settings. System security settings stay as they are, and a driver problem can’t take down the Mac’s other audio.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon
  • macOS 26 or later
  • A network connection to an ST 2110 / AES67 facility network with a PTPv2 grandmaster
